Who We Are

WangZha Team is an independent game studio focused on casual hidden-object puzzle games for mobile platforms. Our debut title, Odd One Out!, challenges players to find the objects that do not belong in beautifully illustrated American culture scenes — from retro diners and baseball stadiums to wild west towns and Thanksgiving dinner tables.

All artwork in our games is original, created for this project. We design every scene, every character and every "odd one" ourselves.
