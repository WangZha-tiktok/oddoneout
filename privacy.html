This Privacy Policy describes how WangZha Team ("we", "our") handles information in connection with our mini game Odd One Out! (the "Game"), which is offered through the TikTok Mini Games platform. By playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. Specifically:

  • No account registration. The Game does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any directly identifying information.
  • Local game progress. Your game progress (cleared rounds, hints and settings) is stored only on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it.
  • No analytics or tracking by us. We do not operate our own analytics servers and do not track your behavior across apps or websites.

2. Third-Party Platform and Advertising

The Game runs on and is distributed through the TikTok Mini Games platform, and uses TikTok's In-App Ads service (including rewarded video ads):

  • When you watch a rewarded video ad, ad serving and any related measurement are handled entirely by TikTok / ByteDance under TikTok's own Privacy Policy. We do not receive or control that data.
  • Your use of the TikTok platform (login state, profile information visible to the platform, etc.) is governed by TikTok's terms and policies, not by this policy.

3. Data Storage and Processing

Game progress data is stored locally on your device. We do not store any player data on servers operated by us. Platform-side data (such as data processed by TikTok's advertising systems) is stored and processed by TikTok / ByteDance, primarily in the United States, in accordance with TikTok's privacy practices.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is a casual puzzle game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the higher age required by local law). Because we do not collect personal information, no such data is stored or shared.

5. Your Choices

  • To delete your game progress, clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game from your device.
  • To understand and control ad-related data processed by TikTok, refer to the privacy settings available in your TikTok app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at marinelfalcon@gmail.com. We typically respond within 2 business days.
